## Engineering community
Central to the School of Engineering’s mission is our commitment to supporting the success of all members of our Engineering community. For our faculty, students, and staff, we foster an environment that is nurturing, welcoming and inclusive of individuals from all backgrounds, perspectives and beliefs.

With opportunities for exceptional [research](https://engineering.stanford.edu/faculty-research) and mentorship and an array of majors and classes, students have the opportunity to get the most out of an experience at Stanford. ## [What is Aeronautical and Astronautical Engineering?](https://aa.stanford.edu/)
At Stanford, the Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics integrates multiple engineering disciplines and focuses on structural, aerodynamic, propulsion, and guidance and control challenges posed by aircraft and spacecraft.
