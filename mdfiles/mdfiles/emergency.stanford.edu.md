## Latest Updates
  * ### Community Advisory - Stanford Campus power outage
Wednesday, December 24, 2025, 1:14 PM PST
On December 24, at 1:18 a.m., the Stanford Campus experienced a brief power outage due to a weather-related PG&E service interruption. Power was restored within 12 seconds.
The outage caused building systems and generators to cycle as designed. Stanford Land, Buildings and Real Estate (LBRE) technicians responded to assess any building system alarms and ensure their full restoration. No outage-related damage has been reported.
The LBRE team has remained on-site to address localized flooding and wind-related impacts to campus, and prepare for any additional storms based on National Weather Service forecasts. 
Those with questions about their research areas or workspaces should contact their local building manager directly. To report critical facility and utility issues, contact the **LBRE Urgent Maintenance Line (650) 723-2281.**
To report an emergency on campus, **dial 9-1-1.**


## About AlertSU
[AlertSU](http://alertsu.stanford.edu/) is the Stanford University emergency notification system. It delivers time-sensitive emergency notifications via phone, email, text-messaging, and outdoor warning sirens to all members of the Stanford community. Current Stanford students, faculty, and staff are automatically registered to receive AlertSU notifications. Emeriti and university affiliates can opt-in through the StanfordYou website. Anyone can opt-in to receive alerts through the [Stanford mobile app](https://uit.stanford.edu/stanford-mobile).
